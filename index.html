Journalist and producer

I’m an NYC-based freelance journalist and producer. I’ve written for Washingtonian, The Chief, Federal Times and more, and I’ve produced, shot and edited video documentaries for the media company Ackerley Creative. Most recently, I was a summer associate producer at the film production company Message Pictures.

I’m finishing my master’s at the Craig Newmark Graduate School of Journalism at CUNY. I earned a BA in Media Studies from the University of Virginia in 2020.
